Career as a Git Repository: Tracking Every Choice You Make

Career
Personal Growth
Open Source
Quality Leadership & Industry Practice
A career is not like CDISC Compliance. It can be like git — honestly tracking every thought, choice, and change you make.
Published

April 21, 2026

Over the past six months, many of my friends went through career changes. Most of them moved in a good direction. I feel lucky to witness this, and it made me think again about my own career and life.

Through conversations with them, I started asking myself:

  • What is the core of my career decisions?
  • What do I enjoy?
  • Where am I right now?

A career is not like CDISC Compliance — there are no standard rules to follow. It is not like a program log either — it will not tell you directly if you are right or wrong. And of course, it does not give you as much freedom as an open source tool.

But it can be like git — honestly tracking every thought, choice, and change you make.

After nearly 15 years in my career, looking back at my own “commit history,” I found that the choices that helped me grow the most were never the safest ones. They were the ones that gave me the space I wanted at that moment — and then I looked for whoever was willing to give me that chance. Haha.

Growth needs space. And space does not come automatically. We have to actively try and challenge ourselves. Yes, there is always a chance of failure.

Today I had a conversation with a manager. He shared a dream he wants to pursue. I told him directly: you should go for it. Even if you only finish part of it, you are already “someone who is living their dream.”

As for me, I enjoy learning, exploring, sharing, and connecting with others. This is not hard, because in the fields I love — open source and data visualization — people are passionate about exactly these things.

Going back to the questions I asked at the beginning, the answer is not complicated: choose the direction that lets you do what you want right now, even if it is not perfect.

Until the next choice comes — and your career commits will be there, honest and recorded.
